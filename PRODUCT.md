# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Hiring managers, recruiters, and engineering leads at companies hiring a senior front-end or full-stack engineer, most often for Shopify and React commerce work. They usually arrive from a resume, LinkedIn, or a job application, skim for a minute or two on desktop or phone, and decide whether Alex is worth an interview.

## Product Purpose

alxdna.dev is Alex Medina's portfolio. It exists to get Alex hired into a senior full-stack role with a front-end focus (Shopify and React). Success is a visitor emailing Alex, downloading the resume, or asking alxdnAI a question about his work.

## Positioning

Fourteen years shipping for the web, the last four building Shopify storefronts for brands everyone recognizes (Barnes & Noble, Netflix, SKIMS, Glossier, MoMA, Peet's Coffee, HAUS Labs, and Indigo) at SDG, including SKIMS as the first enterprise brand on Hydrogen. Alex also designs, founded and built Maps On Maps (Expo and React Native, iOS, Android, and web), and has a background in visual art and jazz guitar. The site itself, including the self-hosted alxdnAI agent, is designed and built by Alex, so the code is part of the portfolio.

## Operating Context

Static single page on GitHub Pages (repo guitarbeard/guitarbeard.github.io); merging to the default branch publishes live. alxdnAI chat widget (alxdnai.js) talks to a self-hosted server at ai.alxdna.dev. alxdna.net 301-redirects to alxdna.dev.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Plain HTML, CSS, and JS, no build step. Semantic markup and shared CSS classes, no inline styles (the public source is read by other engineers).
- Light and dark themes are expected.
- The alxdnAI widget, resume PDF link, and email CTA must keep working.
- Phone number never appears anywhere public.

## Brand Commitments

- Name and wordmark: alxdna. ALXDNA DEV stamp logo (images/alxdna-stamp.svg, favicon.svg) and the alxdnAI head avatar whose mouth opens while typing.
- Client work is shown as logos, not screenshots.
- About photo has a Then/Now switch (images/alxdna.jpg, images/alxdna-now.jpg).
- Copy always uses the Oxford comma.

## Evidence on Hand

- Resume: /Alex-Medina-Resume.pdf.
- Client logos: images/logos/ (8 featured, 12 more). Per-client SDG summaries in the project's logos/sdg-case-study-notes.md.
- Maps On Maps: App Store listing, mapsonmaps.com, screenshots in images/maps-on-maps/.
- Shopify Credly badges (Developing Apps for Shopify, Liquid Storefronts for Theme Developers).
- No testimonials, metrics, or case-study numbers exist; do not invent any.

## Product Principles

1. Specific beats impressive: name the brand, the stack, and what Alex actually did.
2. Sound like a person, not a pitch. Plain, first-person, a little dry.
3. The site is a work sample: its craft and its source code are evidence.
4. One clear next step: email Alex or grab the resume.
