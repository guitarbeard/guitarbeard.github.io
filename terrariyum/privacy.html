TerrariYum! Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

TerrariYum! is a puzzle game made by an independent developer ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why. The short version: we don't ask for your name or email, and we don't run our own servers. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and AdMob collects some device information to do that.

What stays on your device

Your progress (bowls cleared, stars, your cookbook, hints left) and your settings (sound, tilt) are saved only on your phone. We never see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows a short ad between bowls, plus an optional rewarded ad you can watch for a hint. These ads come from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID (the IDFA on iPhone, the Advertising ID on Android)
  • your IP address and approximate location based on it
  • device and app information, such as model, OS version and language
  • how you interact with ads

Google's use of this information is covered by Google's own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • iPhone: the game asks whether it may track you, using Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you say no, the advertising ID is not shared and you see less personalized ads. You can change this later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: Google's consent form appears before any ads load, so you can choose whether ads are personalized.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Remove Ads: a one-time purchase turns off all ads.

Purchases

The Remove Ads purchase is handled by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We don't receive your payment details. We only learn whether the purchase was made, so the game can turn ads off.

Children

TerrariYum! is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we'll help remove it.

Data sharing and selling

We don't sell your personal information and we don't share it with anyone other than as described above for ads and purchases.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it on this page and change the date at the top.
